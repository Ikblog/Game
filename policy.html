Privacy Policy for GameKingdom 2

Last Updated: December 22, 2024

Introduction

Welcome to GameKingdom 2! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our gaming application. We've designed this policy to be straightforward and user-friendly while ensuring compliance with global privacy regulations.

Target Audience

GameKingdom 2 is designed for gaming enthusiasts of all ages. However, users under the age of 13 must obtain parental consent before using the application.

Data Collection and Use

While GameKingdom 2 itself doesn't collect personal information, we use Google AdMob for advertising purposes. Through AdMob, the following data may be collected:

  • Device information (device type, model, operating system)
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • General location data (country/region level)
  • App usage data
  • Ad interaction data

This information is used to:

  • Deliver relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security
  • Comply with technical requirements

Data Storage

Your game progress, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device. No gameplay data is stored on our servers.

Third-Party Services

GameKingdom 2 integrates with Google AdMob for advertising purposes. Please note:

  • AdMob has its own privacy policy and data collection practices
  • You can review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings

Ad Choices

You have control over your ad experience:

  • Reset your advertising ID through your device settings
  • Opt out of personalized advertising in your device's settings
  • Use your device's "Limit Ad Tracking" feature if available

User Rights

As a GameKingdom 2 user, you have the right to:

  • Control personalized advertising through your device settings
  • Delete the app and all locally stored game data at any time
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions or concerns
  • Request information about our privacy practices

Children's Privacy

GameKingdom 2 complies with children's privacy protection laws worldwide. For users under 13:

  • We show only age-appropriate advertisements
  • Personalized advertising is disabled
  • Parents can contact us about any privacy concerns

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted within the app and on our website.
